Himitsu Note日本語

Find what only the two of you share.

Himitsu Note (“secret notebook”) lets you swap profile cards with friends by QR code and shows the hobbies you have in common. For each hobby you choose “Public”, “Show if shared”, or “Private”, so a hobby you are shy about is revealed only to someone who has it too.
