Support

For bug reports and requests, email info@kokotabase.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

FAQ

How do I exchange?
Open “友達” (Friends) and tap “＋” (Add friend) at the top right. One person shows their QR code, the other taps “読み取る” (Scan); without a camera, enter or paste the 8-character code under the QR via “コードを入力” (Enter code). The scanner confirms the name and you both become friends. A code is valid for 10 minutes and one person.
Public / Show if shared / Private?
Public hobbies are visible to all friends. “Show if shared” hobbies appear only to friends who have the same hobby. Private hobbies are only for you. Change them in “書く” (Write).
I can’t find my hobby
Type it in “趣味を追加” (Add hobby). The App searches its own tags, tags added by other users, and suggestions from Wikidata (the open database behind Wikipedia). If nothing matches, tap “〇〇を新しいタグとして追加” to add it. Tags you add can be chosen by others (who added them is not shown).
When do I get notifications?
When a friend adds a hobby and you have something new in common (about a minute after their change), when a friend likes one of your hobbies, and when someone exchanges with you using your QR code. Notifications are on by default. Lock-screen notifications never include hobby names; see which hobby in the “通知” (Notifications) tab at the bottom of the App. Turn each type on or off in Settings → “通知”.
How do likes work?
On a friend’s card, tap the ♡ next to a hobby (or the hobby badge) to like it; tap again to undo. Only the owner of that hobby sees the like.
Notifications don’t arrive
Turn on Settings → Notifications → ひみつノート → Allow Notifications on your iPhone, and check that each type is on in the App’s Settings → “通知”. Signing out stops notifications to that device.
Unfriend, block or report
Open a friend’s card and tap “…” at the top right. We review reports within 24 hours.
Delete my account
Settings (gear on the Card screen) → “アカウントを削除” (Delete account).
